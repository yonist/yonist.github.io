Bic Bac Boe – Privacy Policy

Bic Bac Boe is a tic-tac-toe game for Android (package com.bimburi.tic_tac_toe). It can be played locally against the device or another person, and online against other players. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what your choices are.

Summary

  • The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics.
  • For online play it stores a random identifier generated by the app, the player name you choose, a push-notification token and the games you play.
  • This information is stored in Google Firebase and used only to run online games.
  • Games are deleted automatically after 7 days of inactivity. You can ask us to delete everything else.

Information the app collects

InformationWhat it isWhy
App identifier A random ID (a UUID) created by the app on first launch and kept on your device. It is not your Android ID, advertising ID, phone number or any hardware identifier, and it does not identify you personally. To tell players apart in online games and to deliver notifications to the right device.
Player name The name you type when you first use online play. It can be anything; it does not need to be your real name. Shown to your opponent and in your games list.
Push-notification token A token issued by Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging for this installation. To notify you when it is your turn, when a game ends, and about rematch requests.
Game records For each online game: the board, whose turn it is, the result, the two players' identifiers and names, timestamps, and rematch requests. To run the game between two devices and show your ongoing and finished games.

The app also keeps a few settings only on your device (never uploaded): whether sound is on, an unfinished local game so it can be resumed, and which finished games you have already looked at.

Information the app does not collect

No email address, no contacts, no location, no photos or files, no advertising identifier, no usage analytics and no crash reports. The app requests the Notifications permission so it can tell you when it is your turn; you can refuse it and the app still works.

Where the information is stored and who can see it

Online data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging), operated by Google LLC, on servers in the United States. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms.

Your player name and game records are visible to the people you play against. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

How long it is kept

  • Games are deleted automatically 7 days after their last move.
  • Your identifier, player name and notification token are kept so you can keep playing under the same name. They are deleted on request (see below).
  • Uninstalling the app removes the identifier and settings from your device. A reinstall creates a new identifier; the old one simply stops being used.

Your choices and rights

  • Play offline only. Local games (against the device or a friend on the same phone) send nothing anywhere.
  • Change your name at any time from the online menu.
  • Delete your data. Email [EMAIL] with the player name you use. We will delete your identifier, name, notification token and any remaining games within 30 days. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask a question or two to confirm you are the player in question.
  • Turn off notifications in Android's app settings.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights (for example access, correction or portability under the GDPR). Write to the address above and we will help.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided a player name through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting something new (for example crash reports), this page will be updated and the effective date changed before the new version is released.
